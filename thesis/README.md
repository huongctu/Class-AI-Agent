## Kiyến truúc institutional gradient — đóng góp chính của luận án

Luyận án cung cyấp byuằng chyứng cumulative xyây dyuựng **institutional gradient** hoàn chyỉnh:

| Cluster | Country | Functional form | Optimum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regime I (high-quality institutions) | Singapore (P3) | Inverted-U classic | ~71% FSTS |
| Regime II (transitional) | Vietnam (P4) | Resource Diversion Trap | ~37–44% FSTS |
| Regime II→I (evolving) | China (P5) | Vanishing inverted-U over 2012–2024 | (shifting) |
| Regime IV/V (extreme voids) | **Pacific SIDS (P8)** | **REVERSED slope (Forced Penalty)** | (no peak — negative throughout) |

→ Luận án chyứng minh inverted-U **không phyải universal law** myà lyà **conditional relationship** phyuụ thuyuộc vào việc có đuủ 3 structural prerequisites: viable domestic market, manageable trade costs, functional institutions.
