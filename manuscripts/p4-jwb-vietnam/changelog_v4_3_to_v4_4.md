### v4.3 → v4.4 numerical refresh under editorial review

The reviewer cited several numerical claims from earlier drafts (LM p .060/.017/.029; exporter share 28.2%/18.9%; DAI 2009 β=0.251, DAI 2015 β=0.022, DAI 2023 β=0.176). These reflect the v4.3 listwise convention that retained WBES `-9` codes as numeric data. The verified spec A drops `-9` codes and yields different numbers (LM .128/.018/.051; exporter share 36.0%/18.3%; DAI 2009 β=0.122, DAI 2015 β=0.007, DAI 2023 β=0.108). The §3.2 transparency paragraph documents this refinement; the §4.2 prose reports the verified LM values; the corrigendum stance — verified-pipeline-wins and v4.3 numbers should be treated as superseded — is preserved.
