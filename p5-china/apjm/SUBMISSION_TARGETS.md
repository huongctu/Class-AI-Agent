## Recommended sequencing

If APJM **R&R / desk-reject:** the natural Tier A/B fallbacks (without major rewrite) are **MIR, JIM, IBR, EMFT** — minor adjustments to scope and framing only.

If chị muốn targeting a higher-tier ASAP: **JWB or JIBS** would require strengthened theoretical contribution (especially the dissertation-architecture stream framing — explicit positioning vs prior China IP literature) and possibly an extended sample beyond China for generalisability claims.

If chị muốn faster acceptance: **JABS, IJoEM, EMFT** are realistic 6-12 month target.
